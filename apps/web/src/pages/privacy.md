# Quoter Privacy Policy

**Last updated on November 7th, 2026.**

This document explains what data is collected, how it's used, and how you can delete your data. **If you have any questions/concerns about this Privacy Policy, join our [support server](https://discord.gg/QzXTgS2CNk) OR email [quoter@nickoates.com](mailto:nick@nickoates.com)**.

## Data collected

- The quotes you save, including their text and author. When you use **Quote This** on a message, that's the message's text and its author's Discord username.
- Information about each quote, including when it was created and last edited, the Discord user IDs of whoever created and last edited it, and the message and channel IDs of the original message.
- Each server's ID, and settings like its quote limit.
- Logs, which include the names and IDs of servers Quoter is added to or removed from, and details of errors.

## Data usage

Quoter only uses data to provide its own functionality: saving and displaying quotes. We never use or collect data for marketing and/or analytics, and we don't sell it either.

## Data deletion

When Quoter is removed from a server, that server's data is deleted 30 days later. If Quoter is added back within those 30 days, the data is kept. If Quoter is offline when it's removed, the 30 days start when it's next online.

Quoter takes daily backups of all data, which may include deleted data. These backups are stored for 30 days, after which they are deleted.

You can delete individual quotes with `/delete-quote` or `/delete-own-quote`, and download a server's quotes at any time with `/export`. You can also contact us (refer to the beginning of this document) to manually remove your data.
